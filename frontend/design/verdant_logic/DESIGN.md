# Design System Philosophy: Earth & Ochre

## Visual Direction
This design system has transitioned from a vibrant green palette to a sophisticated, grounded aesthetic rooted in earth tones and warm ochre. The primary focus is on professional stability, warmth, and high-quality craftsmanship. It avoids the clinical feel of pure greys in favor of organic, stone-like neutrals and muted metallic accents.

## Typography
The system utilizes a dual-font approach to balance personality with readability:
- **Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides a modern, geometric clarity that feels contemporary and approachable.
- **Body & Labels:** Be Vietnam Pro is used for all functional text, ensuring high legibility across various screen sizes and information densities.

## Color Palette
The color strategy emphasizes a cohesive "Content" variant, ensuring that colors are harmonious rather than high-contrast.
- **Primary (#658024):** A rich, muted ochre used for key calls to action and brand identification.
- **Secondary (#707c52):** A warm taupe that supports secondary UI elements and interactive components.
- **Tertiary (#389e6c):** A soft olive accent used for subtle differentiation and decorative highlights.
- **Neutral (#77786e):** A warm, stone-grey base that provides a soft background and surface structure, avoiding the harshness of cold neutrals.

## Layout and Geometry
- **Roundedness (2):** Components feature moderate corner radii. This strikes a balance between professional precision and approachable softness, avoiding both sharp industrial edges and overly playful pill shapes.
- **Spacing (2):** The system employs a "Normal" spacing density. This provides a balanced layout that is efficient for data-rich interfaces while maintaining enough white space to prevent visual clutter, moving away from the previous "Spacious" configuration for a more focused user experience.
